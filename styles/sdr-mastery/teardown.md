# Style: sdr-mastery

Measured from one reference across its full length (22:55), an Axis Story edit for the client:

| Video | Length |
|---|---|
| [Wkxg6XCiSwE](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wkxg6XCiSwE) "I've made 100,000 cold calls. Here's what works." | 22:55 |

1920x1080. The numbers come from code (`style.json`, `timeline.json`). The design notes come from
full-resolution frames, and every one of the 53 graphic moments was checked by eye.

**Copy it exactly** (Ayush, 2026-10-13): stock images, third-party screenshots and the client's own
footage are used here as the reference uses them.

## Summary

A clean, bright explainer. **He is on camera about 80% of the time**, one camera, framed close and
centred, with punch-in zooms on the jump cuts. Graphics are **full-screen white pages** with a single
**red accent**, a clean sans for the text and an **italic serif** for spoken lines and headers. Words
**write on as he says them**. Graphics come thick in the first minutes and thin out towards the end.
**No captions.** A music bed runs under the whole video; sound effects are rare.

## Pace over time

| | Value |
|---|---|
| Speech | 217 words a minute, pauses cut to 0.4 s or less |
| Cuts | 6.2 a minute on average. The first minute is busiest (25 cuts), then about 10 a minute to 4:00, then 4-6 a minute to the end |
| Zoom | Hard punch-ins on the jump cuts, alternating in and out (about 1.1x), 4.9 a minute. One slow push-in on the very first shot |
| Transitions | Hard cuts. Graphics cut in or pop in; there are no whips, dissolves or flashy transitions |
| First graphic | 3 s in |
| Time on camera between graphics | Median 19 s, longest 2.5 min |

Full-screen graphics: 37 of them, median 6 s each, most under 10 s; a script page can stay 30 s.

## Sections

1. **Hook (0:00-0:52):** credentials with numbers ("100,000 cold calls... $10 million in pipeline"),
   the promise, then "three parts" with a **Part 1 / Part 2 / Part 3 card**.
2. **Part 1: the list (0:52-6:18).** A **mid-video call to action** at 6:18 ("link in the
   description" on screen over him).
3. **Part 2: the script (6:23-13:31).**
4. **Part 3: doing it consistently (13:31-22:20).**
5. **Recap and call to action (22:20-22:55):** a bullet card of the three parts, the offer, thanks.
   No end screen.

**The part card returns at the start of each part**: the parts done so far and the current one are
readable, and the later ones are blurred.

## Graphics (what the editor will make)

| Type | How it looks | How often |
|---|---|---|
| **Title + list** | White page. Centred sans heading with a **hand-drawn red marker underline**. Numbered list or red-dot bullets below, each line appearing as he says it | The most common, ~10 |
| **Part / stat rows** | A filled red rounded label ("Part 1", "10%-20% will convert", "1% of my calls") joined to a red-outlined box with the text | ~6 |
| **Script page** | Italic serif header ("Let's say someone said:", "I will say:") with a **thick red highlighter bar**, the quote in sans below. Each turn of the call adds on and stays | ~5, the longest graphics |
| **Rule / acronym card** | Soft-shadowed white card, italic serif header with red highlighter + one line. "BANT" in huge black letters on a red bar, with a word under each letter as he says it | ~5 |
| **Illustration** | A recurring navy line-art salesperson on the phone, with an italic serif line under it and a red underline. Sometimes a photo or a stat beside it (cash, a water bottle, "10 of those are interested") | 5 |
| **Screenshots** | Real websites and tools (Apollo, Trellus, a CRM dashboard, a revenue card), full frame or framed | 6 |
| **Words over him** | A short spoken phrase in the lower third, white bold sans mixed with white italic serif, red underline on the key words ("Most people mess up / because they start off wrong") | ~5 |
| **Client footage** | A blurred video-call grid for the first seconds | Opening only |

**Motion:** words fade up one at a time as he speaks; underlines draw on left to right; pages cut in or
scale up quickly with a fade (0.25-1 s, smooth ease); small images slide or pop in with a slight
bounce.

**Colours:** white #FFFFFF / #FAFAFA, dark grey text #313535, red #F44643, navy illustration.

## Captions

**None.** Only the few "words over him" moments above.

## Sound

- **Music:** a bed under the whole video from 0:21 to 22:03, **louder than the Axis Story style**
  (about 14 dB under the voice, against 21 there).
- **Sound effects:** 13 in the whole video (0.6 a minute), mostly low impacts, bunched in the first
  minute on the first graphics.

## Decided with Ayush (2026-10-13)

- **Approved** as the style for SDR Mastery.
- **Illustrations are made for each video.** Keep the look (navy line-art, italic line under it, red
  underline), but draw what that video talks about. The salesperson was specific to the reference.
- **Music:** a track picked to match the reference's bed in feel and level.
- Still welcome from the client: their own footage or screenshots for each opening, and fonts if custom
  (otherwise Inter and Playfair Display Italic).
